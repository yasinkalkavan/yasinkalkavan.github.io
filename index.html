2. Data We Collect

BudgetHome collects the following data in order to provide its features: a) Account and Profile Data • Email address and password — used to create an account and log in. Your password is stored securely by Firebase Authentication; we never see or store it in plain text. • Your display name and the avatar emoji you choose — shown on your profile and in the groups you are part of. • If you sign in with Google or Apple: the name, email address and unique account identifier provided by that service. If you choose to hide your email with Apple, the relay address provided by Apple is used. We never see your Google or Apple password. b) Financial Transaction Data For every transaction you enter: amount, category, note (optional), transaction date, income/expense type and, if you entered it in another currency, the original amount and currency. If you set up a recurring transaction, its settings (amount, category, frequency, end date, etc.) are also stored. c) Debt Tracking Data If you use Debt Tracking, the person's name you enter, amount, unit, direction (owed to you / you owe), due date, reminder setting, note and recorded payments are stored. Names you enter here are only your own records; they are not shared with those people and no notification is sent to them. d) Settle Up and Family Group Data When you use the app's "Settle Up" (shared expense/debt splitting) or "Family Group" feature to create a group, or join one using an invite code: • Your name and the avatar emoji you chose are shared with the other members of that group • The expense, payment, income/expense and debt records you add within that group (amount, description, category, date, who paid and how it was split) can be viewed by the other members of the same group • Likewise, you can view the records other members of that group add within it • You can add people who don't use the app to a group with just a name and an emoji; these names are visible to the other group members. When adding other people's names, please make sure they are aware of it. This sharing is limited to the groups you are part of and to data added within that group; your personal (non-group) transactions and debt tracking records are never shared with any other user. You can stop sharing data with a group's members at any time by leaving that group. e) Data Stored Locally on Your Device The following are kept only on your device and are never sent to us or any third party: language and currency preference, dark mode preference, monthly budget limit, category budgets, categories you create, notification and reminder preferences, app lock settings (see section 11) and the summary shown on the home screen widget. Notifications and reminders are scheduled on your device. f) Advertising Data The app displays ads via Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may collect an Advertising ID, device information, approximate location (via IP), and ad interaction data. This data is used to show and measure ads and, if you consent, to personalize them. • Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown Google's consent (GDPR) form before any ads are loaded, where they set their preference. • On iPhone, your advertising identifier is used only if you allow the app's tracking request (App Tracking Transparency). • To reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads, use Settings > Google > Ads on Android, or Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking on iPhone. g) Currency Rate Requests When you enter a transaction in another currency or change your currency, the app requests current or historical exchange rates from the open-source Frankfurter service (api.frankfurter.app). These requests contain only currency codes and a date; no information about your account or transactions is sent. As with any internet request, your IP address may be visible to this service.

6. Security of Your Data

Your data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and protected by Firebase Firestore security rules; only your own authenticated account can access your personal data, and only the members of a group can access that group's data. Group records can be edited or deleted only by the person who added them. That said, no data transmission over the internet or cloud storage can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

7. Data Retention

Your data is retained until you delete your account. • Personal transactions you delete are kept in "Recently Deleted" for 30 days, during which you can restore them or delete them permanently. After 30 days they are permanently deleted automatically. • You can delete your account instantly in the app via Settings > Delete Account, or request deletion at bbudgethome@gmail.com. When your account is deleted, your personal transactions, recurring transactions, debt records and profile are permanently deleted. • If you are a member of a Settle Up or Family group, deleting your account removes your membership; if no other member remains, the group is deleted entirely. If other members remain, the group's historical records are kept for them so the group's shared balances stay correct, but your name is replaced with a "Deleted User" label.

It is hashed one-way (SHA-256) together with a randomly generated salt, and is kept only on your own device; it is never sent to us or to any server.
